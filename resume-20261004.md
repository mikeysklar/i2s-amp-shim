## What the board is

Passive shim from a Feather's 3V/GND/A0/A1/A2 to the Adafruit MAX98357 I2S amp (#3006).
17.51 × 24.88 mm, single layer F.Cu, no vias, no parts besides the two sockets.

| Feather | Amp | Route |
|---|---|---|
| 3V | Vin (J2.1) | own lane north, east into J2.1 |
| GND | GND (J2.2) | own lane north, east into J2.2 |
| A0 | BCLK (J2.6) | straight |
| A1 | LRC (J2.7) | straight |
| A2 | DIN (J2.5) | east under J2's south end, north under J2 body, west into J2.5 on its row |
| — | SD, GAIN | open (9 dB, (L+R)/2 mono) |

CircuitPython: `audiobusio.I2SOut(bit_clock=board.A0, word_select=board.A1, data=board.A2)`.

## Process and design rules (user's current house style)

- Bantam mills the outline; xTool F1 Ultra fiber laser ablates copper. Bottom silk is burned with the laser.
- Traces 1.0 mm (pad width, "as thick as possible"), ≥0.6 mm between traces, 0.4 mm chamfers, no right angles.
- **No GND pour.** GND is a routed trace. The rest of F.Cu is an **unconnected (no-net) fill**, 0.5 mm gap to all copper, 0.5 min width, inset 0.5 mm from the outline. Fill must form a **closed ring** around the board (XCS trips on open fill) — that's why the east edge was widened.
- Both sockets are right-angle flat SMT, overhanging: J1 75% off the west edge, J2 61% off the east edge.
- Amp plugs in standing vertical, parts facing east (away from Feather), Vin north. Feather stands vertical too, parts facing west, USB end north.
- Front silk: end labels only (past each end of each pin row): `Feather`, `3V`, `A2` at J1; `VIN`, `LRC` at J2; pin-1 dots. 0.8 mm Data 70. Nothing in the strip under the socket legs.
- Back silk (mirrored): "Feather / I2S Amp / Shim", a boxed A0→BCLK / A1→LRC / A2→DIN table, date stamp.
- No mounting holes. Rounded 3 mm corners.

## How to rebuild

From `kicad/`, with KiCad closed (or close without saving first):

```sh
python3 gen_sch.py                                   # schematic (fixed symbol UUIDs)
python3 gen_render_fps.py                            # render-only Feather/amp footprints
/Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources/bin/freecadcmd make_3d.py   # flat SMT socket .wrl models
/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/Current/bin/python3 build_pcb.py
/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/MacOS/kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-all --schematic-parity --format json -o /tmp/drc.json i2s-amp-shim.kicad_pcb
```

Expected DRC: 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 parity; one `isolated_copper` warning (the no-net fill, by design) plus TrueType `text_thickness` noise.
`build_pcb.py` also regenerates `etch/etch-FCu-MIRRORED-1to1.pdf` and `etch-FCu-reference.pdf`.
Rebuilding resets any plot settings saved from the KiCad GUI (user had changed DXF units/format/drill shape).

## Files

- `kicad/build_pcb.py` — source of truth for the board. Geometry constants at top, everything derived.
- `kicad/gen_sch.py` — J1 (Conn_01x05_Socket), J2 (Conn_01x07_Socket); nets /3V3 /GND /BCLK /LRC /DIN.
- `kicad/footprints/i2s-amp-shim.pretty/` — `Feather_RtAngle_Socket_SMD_1x05`, `Amp_RtAngle_Socket_SMD_1x07` (pads copied from sdio-shim's `header-rt-ang-smd`: 2.51 × 1.0 at x=±6.7), and render-only `Render_Feather_RP2350_on_J1`, `Render_MAX98357_on_J2` (board_only, excluded from BOM/pos, no pads; placed as VIS1/VIS2 at the J1/J2 origins).
- `kicad/make_3d.py` — FreeCAD: stock THT right-angle socket → body kept, legs cut at board surface, flat feet added → VRML (black body, gold pins; STEP from freecadcmd loses colours).
- `kicad/gen_render_fps.py` — computes KiCad model offset/rotate for Adafruit boards + male headers. KiCad applies `Rz(-rz)·Ry(-ry)·Rx(-rx)`; decomposition handles gimbal lock (the headers hit it).
- `kicad/3dmodels/` — Adafruit STEPs (MIT, Adafruit_CAD_Parts), KiCad header/socket STEPs (CC-BY-SA), generated `.wrl`.
